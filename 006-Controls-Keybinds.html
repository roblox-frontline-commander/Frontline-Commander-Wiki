01

The Reticle

On a controller there is no mouse pointer. Instead, a reticle sits fixed at the center of your screen and the left stick moves the battlefield underneath it. Whatever the reticle is over is what your buttons act on — select it, order onto it, build on it. Park the reticle near an enemy and the targeting computer locks on and shows its intel card; park it on one of your own selected units and the camera starts tracking that unit until you move the stick again.

Touch the sticks and the reticle appears; touch the mouse and it hands back to the pointer. Both inputs stay live at all times.

04

The Five That Matter

T1

A selects, X acts, B backs out. Ninety percent of command is those three buttons at the reticle.

T2

Hold A and sweep. The paint ring is the fastest way to gather a battlegroup — everything it touches stays yours, even after the ring moves on.

T3

Hold X before a fight, not during one. A shaped line with its facing set beats a blob arriving one tank at a time — armor is thickest on the front plate.

T4

Double-LB is your panic button. Everything you own, selected, instantly — then X on the threat.

T5

Let the camera drive. Park the reticle on your spearhead and tracking follows the advance while your hands stay free for orders.
